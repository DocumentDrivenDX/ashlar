## Design disposition

Keep the inline atomic guard for the next ingest candidate: it removes redundant predecessor I/O while preserving complete before-carrier refusal and full new/history qualification. Retain canonical exact carriers, permanent source/journal/tombstone roles, typed endpoints, hash+full-key reads and immutable manifests. The smaller-file overlay alternative remains experimental; no changed canonical-schema revision or external engine support claim follows from this run.

The dominant current operation still accesses most of the32GBedge parent for uniformly scattered updates. Next compare its actual physical join/read plan with narrow source-key admission and carefully bounded source-join shaping on an already-qualified immutable pair before another write. Measure operator/remote/CPU contributions and include source materialization/maintenance/CDF, not just MERGE execution. Separately reduce repeated per-batch metadata/telemetry work only where immutable source/role qualification can be reused and exact submitted-handle custody retained. Do not discard permanent history, full changed-carrier proof or production fencing to chase60s.

Singleton reads for new fourth-batch keys are being measured at E6/E7 with uncached full-carrier responses; those terminal results are a separate iteration. Caller250ms/cold/concurrency, sustained/burst ingest and actual1B/5B remain unproved or failed. UC Delta is selected regardless of these provisional misses; Fabric stays a bounded projection and PuppyGraph/GraphFrames interoperability retains its previously scoped evidence.
